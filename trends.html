  1. Pervasive Computing, also called Ubiquitous computing, is a recent trend where computing technology is embedded into everyday items to grant those devices additional functionality. The embedded technology is designed in a manner that allows the device to transmit and receive data while being concealed/ invisible from the people around them.

    Areas where pervasive computing is applied:

    Retail, Healthcare, Tracking (Speed, Location, etc.), Car Information System, etc.

  2. An electronic menu, E-menu, “is an interactive restaurant ordering system” (http://emenu.su/en/) that displays an image and description of the advertised product(s) on a digital device such as a tablet, phone, laptop, etc.

    With the revamp of restaurants and physical stores after Covid-19 restrictions, saving time, money, increasing operations and interest for businesses, grew exponentially. E-menus allow a more modernized way of tending to customers while continuing to fulfill their operations.

  3. “A Mini PC is a small-sized, inexpensive, low-power, legacy free desktop computer…” (https://www.alliedmarketresearch.com/mini-pcs-market-A13152)

    Minicomputers are the alternative option to regular sized PCs. Though not as powerful, these inexpensive computers are capable of basic computer tasks such as “web browsing, accessing web-based applications, document processing, and audio/video playback.” (https://www.alliedmarketresearch.com/mini-pcs-market-A13152).

    With Covid-19 impacting all industries, many companies opted for a work from home design. This change has made it mandatory for staff to own devices such as Desktop PCs, laptops, and/or tablets. Minicomputers allow for almost the same work capabilities as larger devices but at a significantly budgeted cost.
